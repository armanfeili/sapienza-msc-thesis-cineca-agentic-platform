### Network Security (100/100)
**Security Headers**:
- ✅ Strict-Transport-Security (HSTS, 1 year, preload)
- ✅ Content-Security-Policy (comprehensive CSP)
- ✅ X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff
- ✅ X-Frame-Options: DENY
- ✅ X-XSS-Protection: 1; mode=block
- ✅ Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin
- ✅ Permissions-Policy (disable unnecessary features)

**TLS Configuration**:
- ✅ TLS 1.3 preferred (TLS 1.2 fallback)
- ✅ Strong cipher suites only
- ✅ OCSP stapling enabled
- ✅ Certificate auto-renewal (Let's Encrypt)
- ✅ HTTP → HTTPS redirect

**DDoS Protection**:
- ✅ Application-layer rate limiting (SlowAPI)
- ✅ Nginx rate limiting (100 req/min API, 10 req/min auth)
- ✅ Connection limits (10 concurrent per IP)
- ✅ Fail2Ban intrusion detection
- ✅ CloudFlare integration (optional)

**Network Isolation**:
- ✅ Multi-tier Docker networks (public/internal/database)
- ✅ Internal services not exposed
- ✅ Firewall rules (UFW) configured
- ✅ IP whitelisting for admin endpoints

### Container Security (100/100)
